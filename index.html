Ansh Nagda

anshnagda (at) berkeley (dot) edu

I am a PhD student in the Theoretical Computer Science group at UC Berkeley, where I am advised by Prasad Raghavendra. Before starting graduate school, I was an undergrad at the University of Washinton.

My primary research interests lie in theoretical computer science, with a broad focus on complexity theory and approximation algorithms. Recently, I have been thinking more about problems in quantum computing.
